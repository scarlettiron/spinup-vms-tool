# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We, as members, contributors and maintainers, pledge to make taking part in Vmynt a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive
and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being kind, patient and respectful, especially to people who are new to the project or to
  programming
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes, apologizing to those affected, and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community, not only for ourselves

Examples of behavior that is not acceptable:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their
  explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in every project space (the repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions and the website), and when someone officially represents the project in public.

## Enforcement responsibilities

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and will take
appropriate and fair action in response to any behavior they find inappropriate, threatening,
offensive or harmful. That includes removing, editing or rejecting comments, commits, code,
issues and other contributions that don't follow this code of conduct, and explaining why when
it is appropriate to do so.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer,
Scarlett A. Scott ([@scarlettiron](https://github.com/scarlettiron)), privately through GitHub.
Don't raise it in a public issue or thread. Every report will be reviewed promptly and fairly,
and the privacy of the person reporting will be respected.

## Enforcement

The maintainer will follow these steps in deciding the consequences of any action they judge to
break this code of conduct:

1. **Correction.** For inappropriate language or other unprofessional behavior: a private,
   written warning explaining what happened and why it was inappropriate. A public apology may
   be requested.
2. **Warning.** For a single serious incident or a series of actions: a warning with
   consequences for continued behavior, including no interaction with the people involved for a
   set period of time.
3. **Temporary ban.** For a serious violation, including sustained inappropriate behavior: a
   temporary ban from any interaction or public communication with the community.
4. **Permanent ban.** For a pattern of violations, harassment of an individual, or aggression
   toward or disparagement of groups of people: a permanent ban from any public interaction
   within the community.
